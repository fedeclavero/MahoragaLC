# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- Initial release of **Mahoraga LC** (`Fede.MahoragaLC`).
- Added Divine General Mahoraga (`3.75m` / `1.5x` player stature) with custom sculpted HDRP 3D model, 8 baked animations, and 8 custom audio assets.
- Implemented server-authoritative 5-state AI (`Patrolling`, `Investigating`, `Chasing`, `Attacking`, `Adapting`), 6-category Wheel of Adaptation, locked-door forcing, dynamic proximity/occluded boss theme, Ship Terminal Bestiary entry (`info mahoraga`), and full BepInEx configuration.
